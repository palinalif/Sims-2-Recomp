# Work claims

One contributor (human or agent), one function, at a time. Add a row **in your
draft claim PR** — merging the claim PR is what grants the claim. Delete your
row when your function merges to verified, or when you abandon it.

A claim covers one RVA (or one explicitly bounded call-graph slice). If you
need to touch a function outside your claim to unblock it, open an issue
rather than quietly editing it.
